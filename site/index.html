How much the arrival date moved

Each row is one bird. The hollow dot is where its arrival date sat in the early window; the filled dot is the late window. A line running leftwards means the bird now arrives earlier in the year.

    The two resident birds sit far to the left, and that is not a mistake. A bird that is here all year has already been seen thousands of times by mid-January, so this method says it “arrives” in January. For them, only the length of the line means anything, and it should be nothing.

    What the control birds say

    California Towhee and Oak Titmouse hold the same few acres all year. Neither can possibly arrive earlier. So whatever shift this method reports for them is the method drifting, and that much of the warbler shift is not news about birds.
